# Privacy Policy for Incomatic

_Last updated: June 30, 2026_

Incomatic ("the app," "we," "our") is a take-home pay calculator for iOS. This
policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

**Location.** Incomatic requests access to your device's location, with your
permission, solely to detect your work state and pre-fill it in the calculator.
Location data is used momentarily on your device to determine your state and is
not stored, logged, or transmitted to our servers beyond what's needed to complete
that lookup. You can decline location access and select your state manually instead.

**Financial and payroll information you enter.** To calculate your take-home pay,
the app sends the figures you provide — earnings, pay frequency, filing status,
W-4 details, state/county, pre-tax benefit elections, and retirement contribution
percentages — to our calculation backend so it can compute your paycheck breakdown
and return it to the app.

- If you are **not signed in**, the calculation is processed to produce your result
  and is **not** saved to any personal history. Our server logs record only
  non-identifying metadata about a request — such as the country, state, tax year,
  and pay frequency, and whether a bonus or hourly input was included — and
  deliberately **do not** record the salary or dollar amounts you enter, or the full
  contents of your request.
- If you **sign in** (see "Account information"), the calculation you run — the
  inputs and the computed results — is saved to your private calculation history so
  you can revisit it later. You can delete any saved calculation from within the app
  at any time.

**Account information.** Signing in is **optional** and is used only to save and
sync your calculation history; the calculator is fully usable without an account.
Incomatic uses **Sign in with Apple**, so we never receive or handle your Apple
password. When you sign in, our backend stores a record for your account containing
your Apple user identifier, the display name you choose to share (Apple provides
this only on your first sign-in), and account timestamps (when the account was
created and last used). **We do not store your email address** — Sign in with Apple
lets you keep it private (including via Apple's "Hide My Email" relay), and our backend
does not retain it. Your saved calculations are stored privately against your Apple
user identifier and are only accessible when you are signed in.

## Information We Do Not Collect

Incomatic does not use third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking
technologies. We do not collect your contact list or device advertising identifiers,
and we do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

## How Information Is Used

Financial figures you enter are used exclusively to generate the paycheck breakdown
displayed in the app and, if you are signed in, to save that breakdown to your
personal history. Location is used exclusively to pre-fill your work state. Account
information is used exclusively to authenticate you and associate your saved
calculations with your account.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your inputs are transmitted to our own calculation backend
over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection and are not shared with third parties. Sign in
with Apple is provided by Apple; your use of it is also governed by Apple's privacy
policy.

## Data Security

All communication between the app and our backend is encrypted in transit via HTTPS.
In addition, the salary and dollar amounts you enter are never written to our server
logs, and — when you are not signed in — your requests are not associated with any
persistent user identifier. Saved calculations are stored privately and are only
returned to your own authenticated account.

## Children's Privacy

Incomatic is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
information from children under 13.

## Your Choices

- You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in iOS Settings; the app
  will fall back to manual state selection.
- You can use the calculator without signing in, in which case no calculations or
  account data are stored.
- If you have signed in, you can delete individual saved calculations at any time
  from the History screen, which removes them from our backend.
- You can permanently delete your account at any time from the account screen (tap
  your profile, then **Delete Account**). This erases your account record and all of
  your saved calculations from our backend and cannot be undone.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app's features change. The "Last updated" date above
will reflect the most recent revision.
